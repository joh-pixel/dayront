## Understanding Audio Reversal

### What Is Audio Reversal?

Audio reversal plays a sound backwards – the last moment becomes the first, and the first becomes the last. It's like playing a video in reverse, but for sound.

### How It Works

When you reverse audio:
1. The audio file is read (locally, in your browser)
2. The waveform is flipped
3. The result is a new file that plays backwards

**Key characteristics of reversed audio:**
- Speech becomes unintelligible (unless reversed again)
- Music becomes dreamy and unfamiliar
- Rhythms and beats are disrupted
- Familiar sounds become alien

### Reversed Audio vs. Original

| Feature | Original Audio | Reversed Audio |
|---------|----------------|----------------|
| Speech | Intelligible | Unintelligible (sounds like a foreign language) |
| Music | Recognizable | Dreamy, unfamiliar |
| Rhythm | Natural | Disrupted |
| Direction | Forward | Backward |
| Emotional impact | As intended | Eerie, mysterious |

If you're reversing private recordings, interviews, or unreleased music, uploading to a cloud tool means potential exposure.

Audio files contain metadata about you and your device. Cloud tools can access it.
