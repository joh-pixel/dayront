# How to Prepare Your Video for Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts: A Privacy‑First Guide

Vertical video dominates social media. But raw footage is rarely ready for the feed – it’s too long, too large, or in the wrong format. Here’s how to turn any clip into a social‑media‑ready masterpiece using only Dayront’s browser tools. No uploads, no account, no watermark.

## Step 1: Trim the Fat

Instagram Reels and TikTok have time limits. Use the **Video Cutter** to trim your clip to the perfect length. For TikTok, aim for 15–30 seconds; for Reels, 30–60 seconds.

<img src="https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1611162618071-b39a2ec055fb?w=800&q=80" alt="Smartphone filming vertical video" width="800" height="534" class="rounded-xl my-6" loading="lazy" />

## Step 2: Crop to 9:16 Aspect Ratio

Most social platforms prefer a 9:16 vertical format. If you shot horizontally, use the **Video Cropper** to reframe. Choose “9:16” and position the subject in the center.

## Step 3: Speed It Up (or Slow It Down)

Attention spans are short. Use the **Speed Changer** to increase the pace by 10–20% for a dynamic feel, or slow down a key moment for drama.

## Step 4: Compress the Video Size

Platforms compress your video anyway, but uploading a huge file takes forever. Use the **Video Compressor** to reduce file size while keeping quality high. A 100 MB file can often become 20 MB with no visible loss.

<img src="https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1611162616475-46b635cb6868?w=800&q=80" alt="Video compression concept on screen" width="800" height="534" class="rounded-xl my-6" loading="lazy" />

## Step 5: Optimize the Audio

Don’t forget sound! Use the **[Volume Booster](/tools/volume-booster)** to make sure your voice or music is loud enough for phone speakers. Then run it through the **[Audio Compressor](/tools/audio-compressor)** to even out levels.

## Step 6: Clean Metadata for Privacy

Toggle **“Clean File Privacy Before Download”** to remove GPS, device, and author data. Critical if you’re posting from a location you don’t want public.

### Social Media Video Processing Chart

| Platform | Ideal Length | Aspect Ratio | Max File Size |
|----------|--------------|--------------|---------------|
| TikTok   | 15–60 sec    | 9:16         | 287.6 MB (iOS) / 72 MB (Android) |
| Instagram Reels | 15–90 sec | 9:16         | 4 GB (but keep < 100 MB for speed) |
| YouTube Shorts | 15–60 sec | 9:16         | 128 GB (but keep small) |
| LinkedIn | 3 sec–10 min | 1:1 or 16:9  | 5 GB |
