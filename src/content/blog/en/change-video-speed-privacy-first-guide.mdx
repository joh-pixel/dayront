## Understanding Video Speed Changes

### Speed vs. Frame Rate

When you change video speed, two things happen:

**1. Playback speed changes** – The video plays faster or slower.
**2. Frame rate handling** – The tool must decide how to handle the original frames.

**Critical distinction:**
- **Simple speed change (no interpolation):** Frames are either dropped (speeding up) or duplicated (slowing down). Fast, but motion can appear choppy.
- **Interpolated speed change:** The tool creates new in‑between frames. Slower, but motion is smoother. Dayront's Video Speed Changer uses smart interpolation for quality results.

### Speed Multipliers Explained

| Speed | Effect | Best For |
|-------|--------|----------|
| 0.25x | 4x slower | Extreme slow motion |
| 0.5x | 2x slower | Dramatic slow motion |
| 0.75x | Slightly slower | Emphasis, comprehension |
| 1.0x | Original | No change |
| 1.25x | Slightly faster | Energy boost, time save |
| 1.5x | Moderately faster | Condensing tutorials |
| 2.0x | Double speed | Quick review |
| 3.0x+ | Very fast | Timelapses, skimming |
