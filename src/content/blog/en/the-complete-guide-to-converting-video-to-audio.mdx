Have you ever downloaded a video just for its soundtrack? Or recorded a lecture that you only need the voice track from? Converting video files to pure audio is one of the most common media tasks – and with Dayront you can do it **entirely in your browser**, without ever uploading your videos anywhere.

In this massive guide we’ll walk through every video‑to‑audio conversion scenario, explain the technical details, and show you how to get the best possible quality.

## Why Convert Video to Audio?

- **Save space** – MP3 files are typically 10‑20× smaller than video.
- **Listen on the go** – Podcasts, music, or recorded meetings are easier to consume as audio.
- **Edit more easily** – Audio editors are simpler and faster than video editors.
- **Privacy** – Many people store sensitive videos; converting to audio keeps the content while removing the visual element.

## Special Cases: Extracting Audio from MOV, MKV, and More

### iPhone MOV Files

MOV containers from iPhones often contain AAC audio. Our converter automatically detects the codec and extracts the highest quality stream. No re‑encoding happens unless you choose a different output format.

### MKV with Multiple Audio Tracks

When an MKV file has several audio streams (e.g., commentary, stereo, surround), the tool will select the default one. For now, you can’t choose which track to extract – but we’re adding multi‑track support soon.

### WebM Audio Extraction

WebM files usually contain Opus or Vorbis audio. Converting to MP3 will re‑encode the stream, but we use a high‑quality encoder to maintain the original quality.
