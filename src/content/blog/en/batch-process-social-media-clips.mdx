If you’re a content creator, you probably produce multiple clips a day for different platforms. Editing them one by one is a massive time sink. **Batch processing** is the answer – and with Dayront, you can do it privately, right from your browser.

## What Is Batch Processing?

Batch processing means applying the same edits to multiple files at once. For example, you might want to:
- Trim the first 2 seconds from 10 interview clips.
- Convert 5 WAV files to MP3.
- Compress 8 large videos to smaller sizes.

Instead of repeating the steps for each file, you upload them all, choose your settings, and let the tool do the work.

## The Privacy‑First Advantage

Most batch processing tools are cloud‑based, meaning you upload your raw footage to a third‑party server. That’s a security risk, especially for unreleased content. Dayront’s batch tools run **locally in your browser**, so your files never leave your device.
