## Understanding Audio Extraction

### What Is Audio Extraction?

Audio extraction is the process of separating the audio track from a video file. The video contains both visual and audio data; extraction isolates just the audio portion.

### How It Works

When you extract audio:

1. The video file is read (locally, in your browser)
2. The audio stream is identified
3. The audio is copied without re-encoding (lossless) or re-encoded to a new format
4. The result is a standalone audio file

**Key distinction:**
- **Lossless extraction:** Audio is copied exactly as it exists in the video. Zero quality loss.
- **Re-encoding:** Audio is converted to a different format (e.g., MP3). Minor quality loss possible.

### Common Formats

**Video formats you can extract from:**
- MP4 (most common)
- MOV (Apple)
- WebM (web)
- AVI (legacy)
- MKV (archival)

**Audio formats you can extract to:**
- MP3 (universal compatibility)
- WAV (lossless, for editing)
- M4A/AAC (Apple, YouTube)
- OGG (open-source)
- FLAC (lossless compressed)

## Privacy: Why Local Extraction Matters

When you use cloud-based audio extractors, your video files are uploaded to a third-party server. This creates significant risks:

**1. Full Content Exposure**
Your entire video – visuals and audio – is accessible to the service provider. For sensitive content (interviews, unreleased material, personal videos), this is unacceptable.

**2. Data Retention**
Many services keep copies of uploaded files indefinitely. Your content could be stored, analyzed, or shared without your knowledge.

**3. AI Training**
Some services use uploaded content to train their algorithms. Your voice and video could become part of a machine learning model.

**4. Metadata Leakage**
Video files contain extensive metadata: camera model, GPS coordinates, software used, author name. Cloud tools can access all of it.

**Dayront's browser tools eliminate these risks:**
- Files stay on your device
- Processing happens locally with WebAssembly
- "Clean File Privacy" strips metadata before download
- No accounts, no cookies, no tracking
