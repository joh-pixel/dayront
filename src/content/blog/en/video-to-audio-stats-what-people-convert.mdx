Ever wondered which video format gets converted the most? How large are the files people process? We crunched anonymised numbers from Dayront (only counting **number of conversions**, never looking at file contents) to bring you these fascinating trends.

> **Privacy note:** All data is fully anonymous – we count only the format and file size ranges, never the names or contents of files.

## Most Converted Video Formats

Over the past month, here’s the breakdown of input video formats:

| Format | Share of Conversions |
|--------|----------------------|
| MP4    | 64%                  |
| WebM   | 14%                  |
| MOV    | 10%                  |
| MKV    | 7%                   |
| AVI    | 4%                   |
| Other  | 1%                   |

<Image src={imgvideotoaudiosta2} alt="Chart showing conversion format shares" width="800" height="534" class="rounded-xl my-6" loading="lazy" />

MP4 dominates by a huge margin – no surprise given that smartphones, drones, and most cameras record in MP4. WebM has grown thanks to YouTube rips and web downloads, while MOV remains popular among iPhone users.

## Average File Sizes

We grouped input files by size bucket (in MB):

| Size Range       | Percentage |
|------------------|------------|
| 0–10 MB          | 28%        |
| 10–50 MB         | 41%        |
| 50–200 MB        | 22%        |
| 200–500 MB       | 7%         |
| 500 MB+          | 2%         |

<Image src={imgvideotoaudiosta3} alt="Bar chart of file sizes" width="800" height="450" class="rounded-xl my-6" loading="lazy" />

The sweet spot is **10–50 MB** – typically short videos under 5 minutes. This range converts quickly even on mobile devices, and the resulting MP3 files are usually under 5 MB.

## Why People Extract Audio

We asked users (optional survey) why they convert video to audio. Here are the top reasons:

1. **Save music/soundtrack** (38%)
2. **Extract voice recording / lecture** (27%)
3. **Reduce storage** (18%)
4. **Privacy** – remove visual content (12%)
5. **Other** (5%)

The privacy angle is notable: over 10% of users specifically mentioned that they convert to audio because they don’t need the video and want to avoid accidentally sharing it.

## Geographic Insights

While we don’t track location, language preferences give a rough distribution:

- English: 45%

- Japanese: 7%
