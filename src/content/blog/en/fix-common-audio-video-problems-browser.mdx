## Part 1: Fixing Common Audio Problems

### Problem 1: My Audio Is Too Quiet

You recorded a podcast or voiceover, but the volume is so low that listeners have to crank up their speakers. This is one of the most common issues, and it's easy to fix.

**Solution:** Use the **[Volume Booster](/tools/volume-booster)**.

**Steps:**
1. Open the Volume Booster tool.
2. Drag your audio file into the upload area.
3. Set the boost amount. Start with +6 dB; if it's still quiet, try +9 dB or +12 dB.
4. Toggle "Clean File Privacy" if desired.
5. Click "Boost" and download the louder file.

**Pro tip:** If your audio has peaks that are already near -1 dB, adding too much boost will cause clipping (distortion). In that case, use the **[Audio Compressor](/tools/audio-compressor)** first to even out the levels, then boost.

### Problem 2: My Audio Has Background Noise or Hiss

A constant hum, fan noise, or electrical hiss can ruin an otherwise good recording. While Dayront doesn't have a dedicated "noise removal" tool yet, you can dramatically reduce background noise using compression and equalization techniques.

**Solution:** Use the **[Audio Compressor](/tools/audio-compressor)** combined with the **[Audio Cutter](/tools/audio-cutter)**.

**Steps:**
1. First, use the Audio Cutter to remove any sections where there's background noise but no speech (e.g., long silences at the beginning or end).
2. Then, run the file through the Audio Compressor at a level of 3–4. This reduces the dynamic range, making the noise less noticeable relative to the voice.
3. Optionally, use the Volume Booster to raise the overall level.

**Note:** A dedicated noise reduction tool is on the Dayront roadmap. For now, the combination above works surprisingly well for most recordings.

### Problem 3: My Audio Has Uneven Volume (Loud and Quiet Parts)

If one speaker is loud and another is quiet, or if you move closer and farther from the mic, the volume fluctuates. This is exactly what compression is designed to fix.

**Solution:** Use the **[Audio Compressor](/tools/audio-compressor)**.

**Steps:**
1. Open the Audio Compressor tool.
2. Upload your file.
3. Choose a compression level:
   - **2–3** for a natural sound with mild evening
   - **4–5** for moderate compression (best for interviews)
   - **6–7** for heavy compression (best for loud, dynamic content)
4. Click "Compress" and download.

### Problem 4: My File Is in the Wrong Format

You have a WAV file but need MP3, or you have M4A but need OGG. Format conversion is a daily need for creators.

**Solution:** Use the **[Audio Converter](/tools/audio-converter)**.

**Steps:**
1. Drag your file into the converter.
2. Choose the output format (MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG, FLAC).
3. Adjust quality settings if needed (bitrate, sample rate).
4. Click "Convert" and download.

**Common conversions:**
- WAV to MP3 for podcast distribution
- MP3 to WAV for editing in a DAW
- M4A to MP3 for universal compatibility
- FLAC to MP3 for portable devices

### Problem 5: My Audio Speed Is Wrong

You need to slow down a fast talker or speed up a lengthy recording. Changing speed without changing pitch is essential for natural sound.

**Solution:** Use the **[Speed Changer](/tools/speed-changer)**.

**Steps:**
1. Upload your audio file.
2. Set the speed multiplier (e.g., 1.1 for 10% faster, 0.9 for 10% slower).
3. Choose whether to preserve pitch (recommended for voice).
4. Click "Change Speed" and download.

**Use cases:**
- Speed up a 60‑minute meeting to 45 minutes (1.33x).
- Slow down a fast talker to 0.85x for clarity.
- Create a chipmunk effect by increasing speed without pitch preservation.

### Problem 6: I Need to Remove a Section from the Middle

Maybe you said something you shouldn't have, or there's a cough in the middle of an otherwise perfect take.

**Solution:** Use the **[Audio Cutter](/tools/audio-cutter)**.

**Steps:**
1. Upload your file.
2. Use the waveform to find the section you want to remove.
3. Set the start and end points to exclude that section.
4. Click "Cut" to remove it. (Or select "Keep selection" mode to keep only the part you want.)
5. Download the result.

**Tip:** You can make multiple cuts by repeating the process on the output file, or by using the Audio Merger to combine the remaining sections.

### Problem 7: I Need to Combine Multiple Audio Files

You have an intro, main content, and outro as separate files and need them as one.

**Solution:** Use the **[Audio Merger](/tools/audio-merger)**.

**Steps:**
1. Upload all files (intro, main, outro, music, etc.).
2. Drag to arrange the order (top = first).
3. Optionally adjust volume of individual tracks (if supported).
4. Click "Merge" and download.

**Pro tip:** If you want background music to be quieter than the voice, process the music file separately with the Volume Booster (negative boost not supported, but you can start with a quieter music file) or use the Compressor to balance.

## Part 2: Fixing Common Video Problems

### Problem 8: My Video File Is Too Large to Upload

You shot a video in 4K, and now it's 2 GB. Social media platforms have file size limits, and even if they don't, uploading a huge file takes forever.

**Solution:** Use the **[Video Compressor](/tools/video-compressor)**.

**Steps:**
1. Upload your video to the Video Compressor.
2. Choose a target resolution (e.g., 1080p or 720p instead of 4K).
3. Set the bitrate (e.g., 8 Mbps for 1080p, 4 Mbps for 720p).
4. Click "Compress" and download the smaller file.

**Before and after example:**
- 4K video, 1.5 GB, 50 Mbps → 1080p video, 150 MB, 8 Mbps (90% size reduction, minimal visible loss)

### Problem 9: My Video Is in the Wrong Aspect Ratio

You shot horizontally, but TikTok and Reels require vertical (9:16). Or you shot vertical, but YouTube needs horizontal (16:9).

**Solution:** Use the **[Video Cropper](/tools/video-cropper)**.

**Steps:**
1. Upload your video.
2. Select the target aspect ratio (16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 4:5, etc.).
3. Drag the crop area to frame your subject.
4. Click "Crop" and download.

**Important:** Cropping removes part of the image. If you want to keep the entire frame but change the aspect ratio, you'd need to add padding (letterboxing). Dayront's cropper currently focuses on cropping (removing), not padding. For padding, you may need a different tool, but for most social media, cropping is actually preferred because it fills the screen.

### Problem 10: My Video Is Too Long

You need a 15‑second clip from a 10‑minute video for TikTok.

**Solution:** Use the **[Video Cutter](/tools/video-cutter)**.

**Steps:**
1. Upload your video.
2. Set the start and end points for the section you want to keep.
3. Click "Cut" and download just that section.

**Tip:** You can create multiple clips from the same video by repeating the process with different in/out points. There's no need to re‑upload the file each time if the tool supports multiple cuts (check the interface).

### Problem 11: My Video Has No Audio or Wrong Audio

You want to replace the original audio with a voiceover or music track.

**Solution:** Use the **[Audio Merger](/tools/audio-merger)** to combine your new audio with the video's existing audio, or use a video editor (coming soon to Dayront) to replace entirely. For now, the workaround is:

1. Extract the video's audio using **[Audio Extractor](/tools/audio-extractor)**.
2. Edit the audio as needed.
3. Use a video editor (like the free Dayront Video Editor, when available) or a tool like ffmpeg (command line) to merge the new audio with the original video.

**Note:** Dayront is actively developing a full video editor that will support audio replacement. Subscribe for updates.

### Problem 12: My Video Plays Too Fast or Too Slow

You want to create a timelapse or slow‑motion effect.

**Solution:** Use the **[Video Speed Changer](/tools/video-speed-changer)**.

**Steps:**
1. Upload your video.
2. Set the speed (e.g., 0.5x for half speed, 2x for double speed).
3. Choose pitch preservation for audio if needed.
4. Click "Change Speed" and download.

**Use cases:**
- Timelapse: speed up 10x or more for clouds moving.
- Slow motion: slow down to 0.25x for dramatic effect.
- Tutorials: speed up repetitive parts.

### Problem 13: I Need Audio Only from a Video

You want to turn a video into a podcast episode or extract a music track.

**Solution:** Use the **[Audio Extractor](/tools/audio-extractor)**.

**Steps:**
1. Upload your video (MP4, MOV, WebM).
2. Choose output format (MP3 or WAV).
3. Click "Extract" and download.

**This is perfect for:**

- Saving a song from a video (with permission).

### Problem 14: My Video Is in the Wrong Format

Your camera outputs MOV, but you need MP4 for YouTube. Or you have AVI that won't play on your phone.

**Solution:** Use the **[Video Converter](/tools/video-converter)**.

**Steps:**
1. Upload your video.
2. Choose output format (MP4, WebM, MOV).
3. Adjust resolution and bitrate if needed.
4. Click "Convert" and download.

**Common conversions:**
- MOV to MP4 for universal compatibility
- AVI to MP4 for playback on modern devices
- MP4 to WebM for web embedding
- MKV to MP4 for social media

### Problem 15: I Want to Create a GIF from a Video

GIFs are great for reactions, tutorials, and social media engagement.

**Solution:** Use the **[Video to GIF](/tools/video-to-gif)**.

**Steps:**
1. Upload a short video clip (under 10 seconds works best).
2. Set the start and end points.
3. Choose the output size (smaller = smaller file).
4. Adjust frame rate (lower = smaller file).
5. Click "Convert to GIF" and download.

**Tips for great GIFs:**
- Keep it short: 2–5 seconds is ideal.
- Reduce resolution to 480p or lower.
- Use a frame rate of 15 fps instead of 30.
- Add text overlays if possible (coming soon).
