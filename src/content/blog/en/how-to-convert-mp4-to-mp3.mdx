Whether you want to save the soundtrack of a video, extract a voice recording, or just free up space on your phone, converting an MP4 video to MP3 audio is one of the most common media tasks. With Dayront, you can do it **directly in your browser** – no software to install, no file uploads, and no waiting for a server.

In this tutorial we’ll walk through the entire process, from choosing your video to downloading the clean MP3. By the end you’ll be able to convert any MP4 file in seconds, **100% privately**.

## Why Convert MP4 to MP3?

- **Listen on the go** – MP3 files play on virtually every device.
- **Save storage** – A 5‑minute MP3 takes about 5 MB; an MP4 video of the same length can be 50 MB or more.
- **Edit easily** – Audio editors are simpler and faster than video editors.
- **Privacy** – You can extract the audio without sharing the visual content.

## Step 1: Open the MP4 to MP3 Converter

Head over to the [MP4 to MP3 converter](/convert/mp4-to-mp3) on Dayront. The tool loads immediately in your browser – no app to download. Because it uses **FFmpeg.wasm**, the entire conversion engine runs locally on your device.

## Step 2: Select Your MP4 File

You can either:

- **Drag & drop** your video onto the upload area, or
- **Click** the *Browse Files* button and pick an MP4 from your computer.

The file stays in your browser’s memory. It’s never sent over the internet. That’s the core privacy promise of Dayront.

## Step 3: Start the Conversion

Click the **“Start MP4 to MP3”** button. The progress bar will fill up as the audio is extracted. For a typical 5‑minute video, the conversion takes only a few seconds.

> **Tip:** If you want to remove embedded metadata (like location, author, or device info) from the output, toggle the **“Clean File Privacy Before Download”** switch before converting.

Once the processing is done, a **“Download File”** button will appear. Click it, and the MP3 file will be saved directly to your default download folder. The output uses a high‑quality encoder, so your audio will sound excellent.

## What About Large Files?

Dayront can handle videos of any size, but very large files (over 1 GB) may take longer to process and may require a device with enough RAM. For best results, stick to files under 500 MB, especially on mobile devices.

## Troubleshooting

- **Converter not starting?** Make sure you’re using a modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge). The tool requires WebAssembly support.
- **Error message?** Try reloading the page and converting again. If the issue persists, check that your browser isn’t blocking third‑party cookies or service workers.
- **No sound in output?** Some MP4 files contain video only with no audio track. Dayront can only extract audio that actually exists in the video.

## Privacy Reminder

Because everything happens inside your browser, your original video never leaves your device. You can even disconnect from the internet after the page loads and still convert files offline. This makes Dayront ideal for sensitive content like legal evidence, private conversations, or unreleased creative work.
